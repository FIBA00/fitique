# Fitique Design Directions

## Three approaches considered

### 1. Atelier Receipt

**Very Brief Intro:** A refined boutique journal that pairs contemporary commerce with an elevated fashion-editorial sensibility. It feels personal, tactile, and assured rather than trend-chasing.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Soft Utility Wardrobe

**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, calm shopping system that brings the reassuring clarity of a fitting room to a fashion marketplace. Informative product details meet quiet, inviting visual discovery.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Lavender Lookbook

**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile digital lookbook built from generous ivory space, muted color fields, and expressive editorial cropping. It evokes browsing a small luxury boutique with a thoughtful stylist at hand.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen direction — Lavender Lookbook

**Design Movement:** Contemporary fashion editorial informed by lookbook spreads and Japanese retail restraint.

**Core Principles:**

1. **Editorial hierarchy over dashboard density:** Product imagery, meaningful type scale, and considered whitespace lead every view.
2. **Warm tactility:** Soft paper tones, uncoated-card surfaces, fine outlines, and barely-there shadows convey a boutique experience.
3. **Guided confidence:** The Fit Check is a trusted stylistic companion, visually distinct but never overly technical or clinical.
4. **Quietly expressive details:** Hanger-shaped brand mark, rounded capsule labels, and fine plum rules build a memorable system without excess ornament.

**Color Philosophy:** Warm ivory is the generous paper-like ground and makes imagery breathe. Soft sand and taupe provide warmth in functional areas; pale lavender signals personal guidance and AI-assisted confidence. A deep blackberry-plum is reserved for the brand, decisive CTAs, and moments of intent; ink-brown text gives the palette its premium editorial weight.

**Layout Paradigm:** Pages behave like vertical lookbook spreads rather than a centered SaaS grid. On desktop, primary pages use strong asymmetry: editorial copy occupies a narrow left column while visual collections spill farther to the right. On mobile, this becomes a scrollable sequence of framed moments, anchored by a persistent bottom navigation.

**Signature Elements:**

- The **open hanger** brand mark, redrawn in a fine plum line and used as a visual thread.
- **Mist-lavender confidence notes** for Fit Check guidance, recommendations, and saved-personal details.
- **Editorial product captions** set beneath photos with fine divider rules and small uppercase category labels.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Shopping should feel like being assisted by a considerate boutique stylist. Hover states gently reveal product intent; cart, wishlist, and Fit Check actions give clear, immediate feedback. Decisions are always reversible and navigation always offers a natural route back.

**Animation:** Use a 180–260ms plum-tinted ease-out for hovers, drawers, and selection transitions. Product imagery may lift slightly on hover; Fit Check’s analysis state uses a restrained scanning line and step progression. Respect reduced-motion settings; no decorative animation should delay a decision.

**Typography System:** Headings use **DM Serif Display** for graceful, high-contrast editorial moments. UI, product metadata, and body copy use **Manrope** for clarity. Eyebrow labels are compact uppercase Manrope with generous tracking; hero headings use a fluent serif scale and never all caps.

**Brand Essence:** *Fitique is the boutique shopping companion for people who want a more personal, confident way to choose what they wear.*

**Personality:** Attentive, refined, reassuring.

**Brand Voice:** The voice is concise, personable, and certain without being precious. Headlines invite a more confident choice; CTAs speak in first-person intent.

> “Your best-dressed decision begins with a better fit.”

> “Check my fit before I commit.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** A tall, fluid serif wordmark paired with a delicate open-hanger symbol. The logo is a singular plum stroke and never a generic default-font treatment.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Fitique Blackberry — #493551**.

## Style Decisions

- Preserve the supplied reference’s calm ivory, lavender, warm-sand, and deep-plum visual vocabulary while creating an original responsive storefront experience.
- Keep major photography bright and soft; use dark editorial text on light imagery for reliable contrast.
- Favor low-radius controls with capsule navigation and labels; avoid uniform rounded cards.
- Photography should read as one muted boutique shoot: warm daylight, ivory and taupe environments, softened saturation, and soft editorial cropping.
- Fit Check is a mist-lavender stylist companion, using fine plum rules and reassuring editorial notes rather than technical-form language.
- Product cards lead with editorial captions and compact metadata; purchase and Fit Check actions remain useful but visually secondary.
- The catalogue opens with an asymmetric featured edit and softens every product image into the same warm boutique treatment; repeated card actions only surface as quiet service on hover or focus.
